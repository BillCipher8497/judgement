JUDGEMENT (OH HELL): A STRATEGY YOU CAN USE AT THE TABLE
Phase 5 human guide. Rules: 4 players, hit = 10 + bid, miss = 0, dealer bids last and is hooked,
trumps cycle S H D C NT, 13..1..13 ladder.
Code: judgement/policies/human_guide.py (HumanBidder + HumanPlayPolicy = GuideAgent). Numbers come from
experiments/guide/results (s1g_final.json, s1g_table.txt, s3_summary.txt, s3_ablation.txt).

The guide needs no arithmetic beyond counting. You keep track of three things during a round:
  (a) how many trumps have been played (so you know how many are still out),
  (b) which Aces, Kings and Queens have been played,
  (c) which players have shown out of which suit.
Measured value of this memory: +2.34 +- 0.33 pts/game against remembering nothing, and +0.28 +- 0.14 against
remembering only Aces and Kings. Also remembering Jacks adds +0.10 +- 0.10 (not significant).

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HEADLINE (6,000 games per line, CRN, seats rotated, 95% CI, ties for first count as wins)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  One guide player against three TA bots:     -2.01 +- 0.78 pts/game vs a TA bot in that seat, P(win) 0.235 +- 0.011
  One TA+3 bot (the strongest rule bot):      +7.79 +- 0.55,  P(win) 0.337 +- 0.012
  One greedy player ("always try to win"):   -43.32 +- 0.85,  P(win) 0.019 +- 0.003
  => The guide keeps 81% [79%, 82%] of TA+3's edge over greedy play (81% [80%, 82%] against three TA+3 bots).
     For comparison, the TA bot (exact card tracking and probabilities) keeps 85% (vs TA) / 82% (vs TA+3).
  Where the remaining 9.80 +- 0.74 pts/game to TA+3 goes (vs TA; s3_ablation.txt): bidding 5.50 +- 0.66 (swap
  bids under guide play) to 7.72 +- 0.67 (under TA+3 play); play 2.08 +- 0.47 (under guide bids) to 4.31 +- 0.50
  (under bot bids). Bidding is the bigger loss in either order.
  Four guide players at one table score 155.3 +- 0.3 each per game (four TA: 161.8, four TA+3: 166.4); one TA+3
  bot among three guide players: +12.49 +- 0.78 pts/game, P(win) 0.375 +- 0.012.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BIDDING: COUNT POINTS (2 points = 1 trick)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Value of each line = loss per round when that line is deleted, measured on 40,000 held-out bidding
situations (counterfactual playouts on the true deal, the bidder then plays this guide). Per game = x 25.

  B1  Trump rounds: 2 points for every trump, plus 1 more for the trump Ace, King and Queen.
        value: trumps 1.81 +- 0.06 /round; the extra point for A/K/Q 0.27 +- 0.03 /round
  B2  Every side-suit Ace: 1 point. Every side-suit King: 1 point.
        value: 0.37 +- 0.04 /round (the Kings alone: 0.12 +- 0.03)
  B3  No-trump rounds: every Ace is 2 points. (Kings count nothing in NT.)
        value: 0.14 +- 0.02 /round
  B4  Trump rounds: take off 1 point for every 4 cards in the round (1-3 cards: 0; 4-7: 1; 8-11: 2; 12-13: 3).
        value: 0.49 +- 0.06 /round
  B5  Your bid = points / 2, rounded down.
  B6  Earlier bids: each player who bid before you has a fair share of n/4 tricks. If their total is more than
      2 above the sum of their shares, bid 1 less (more than 6 above: 2 less). If it is 2 or more below, bid 1 more
      (6 or more below: 2 more). Example: n = 8, you bid third; the two earlier players' shares are 2 + 2 = 4;
      they bid 7 in total -> 3 above -> bid 1 less.
        value: 0.08 +- 0.03 /round; in full games +1.53 +- 0.38 pts/game (vs TA), +1.70 +- 0.37 (vs TA+3)
        (just under the 0.1/round bar; kept because it is the only line that uses the table)
  B7  Dealer, if the hook forbids your bid: go up 1 if your points were odd, otherwise down 1 (up if you are at 0).
        value: none measurable (0.00 +- 0.01 /round against "always go down"); either way is fine.

  Example: hearts trump, 8 cards, you hold AH 9H 4H, KS 7S, AD, 3C 2C.
     trumps 3 x 2 = 6, +1 for AH = 7; KS 1, AD 1 -> 9; minus 2 (8 cards) -> 7 points -> bid 3.

  Bidding accuracy (held-out situations, the bidder plays this guide; ModelBidder = the fitted bot bidder):
     matches ModelBidder's bid 61.4% of the time, matches the Monte-Carlo oracle bid 57.7% (ModelBidder 62.7%).
     EV loss vs ModelBidder: 0.19 +- 0.05 pts/round overall (trump 0.20 +- 0.06, NT 0.14 +- 0.13).
     In full games the guide bid costs 5.50 +- 0.66 pts/game (0.22 /round) against ModelBidder bids.
  Lines tried and dropped (each bought < 0.1 pts/round): side Queens (<= 0.03), NT Kings (0.02), short suits /
  voids with trumps (0), NT long suits (0), special rules for 1-3 card rounds (0.02-0.03), counting low cards,
  Queens or Jacks (0.02-0.03 together), rounding up (costs 0.3).
  Ceiling: even real-valued per-round-size regressions on the same hand facts lose 0.17 /round vs ModelBidder,
  so the integer count is about as good as these hand facts allow.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PLAY: THE CHECKLIST (use the first rule that applies)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Words:  NEED = your bid minus the tricks you have taken.
        BOSS = a card that no unplayed card of its suit can beat (you hold or have seen every higher A/K/Q);
               once no trumps are left out, every trump is a boss.
        SURE WINNER = beats the card now winning the trick and either you play last, or it is a boss that
               cannot be ruffed (it is a trump, or it is NT, or no trumps are out, or everyone after you has
               shown out of trumps).
        SURE TRICKS = the number of bosses in your hand.
Value of each rule = full games, 1 guide player vs 3 TA bots, 6,000 games, paired: guide minus guide-without-rule.

  P0  You can no longer make your bid: play your lowest card.  (no effect on your own score)
  At your bid (NEED = 0):
  P1  Following: play your HIGHEST card that does not beat the card now winning. If every card beats it:
      your highest if you play last, otherwise your lowest.                         value +27.46 +- 0.42 /game
  P2  Leading: lead your lowest non-trump.                                            (baseline lead)
  Still needing tricks (NEED > 0):
  P3  You need every remaining trick: follow with your cheapest sure winner (if none, your highest card);
      lead your highest boss, trumps first.                                          value +0.75 +- 0.12 /game
  P4  Following and you hold a sure winner: if NEED = 1 win with your HIGHEST sure winner, otherwise with the
      cheapest.                                                                       value +1.39 +- 0.14 /game
  P5  Following, no sure winner, but a boss beats the card now winning: play it (highest if NEED = 1, else
      cheapest).                                                                      value +1.65 +- 0.26 /game
  P6  Following and out of the led suit: ruff with your lowest trump that beats the card now winning.
                                                                                      value +1.86 +- 0.39 /game
  P7  Following and not winning: if your sure tricks (not counting this card) still cover NEED, throw your
      HIGHEST card; otherwise throw your lowest non-trump.                            value +5.26 +- 0.27 /game
  P8  Leading with a boss: while trumps are out, lead your top trump boss; otherwise a side boss (highest if
      NEED = 1, else cheapest).                                                       value +0.35 +- 0.34 /game (n.s.)
  P9  Leading, no boss: lead your highest non-trump.                                  value +2.47 +- 0.41 /game

  The idea behind P1, P4, P7 (math/NOTES.md M9, Props W and L): spend your dangerous cards on tricks whose
  outcome is already decided, and keep low cards for later.
  Rules tried and dropped: "at your bid in the 13-card round, lead your highest card that an unplayed honour
  beats" (TA+3's Prop L; +0.22 +- 0.12 /game, and -6.1 when used in every round); draw trumps when you hold
  the majority (-0.3); try a non-boss high card when urgent (0); shed only Q or higher (-1.5); low from the
  longest suit in NT (-0.6 vs P9).
  Guide play alone (with bot bids): +3.48 +- 0.61 pts/game vs TA (TA+3: +7.79 +- 0.55); guide play keeps
  92% [91%, 93%] of TA+3's edge over greedy, guide bidding alone (with TA+3 play) 85% [84%, 86%].

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LIMITS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Measured against bots (TA, TA+3), not people; the guide is assumed to be applied without mistakes.
  * Bidding values come from situations where the other seats bid with ModelBidder and play TA+3, and the
    bidding data were made with an earlier guide version (it also remembered Jacks); full-game numbers use the
    final version.
  * Play rules were chosen on separate screen seeds (91,000,000+), then measured on fresh seeds (71,000,000+).
  * Bidding lines were picked on 60,000 training situations and measured on 40,000 held-out ones.

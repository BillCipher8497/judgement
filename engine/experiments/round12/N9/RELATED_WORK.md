Globally normalized conditional likelihood is established structured-prediction
methodology, not a new N9algorithm. See [Lafferty, McCallum and Pereira,
Conditional Random Fields: Probabilistic Models for Segmenting and Labeling
Sequence Data, ICML2001](https://mlanthology.org/icml/2001/lafferty2001icml-conditional/)
and its [primary paper PDF](https://home.cs.colorado.edu/~mozer/Teaching/syllabi/6622/papers/LaffertyMcCallumPereira2001.pdf).

N9applies ordinary conditional log-partition training to a fixed small
card-allocation family and measures a matched-recipe intervention. It does
not claim a new CRF, generic neural convergence guarantee, optimal factor
projection or arbitrary-hand/general-game benefit. Old N2marginal training
versus joint decoding motivates the comparison but is not itself a causal
diagnosis of optimization duration. See PROTOCOL.md for the controlled and
uncontrolled differences and the known-benchmark caveat.

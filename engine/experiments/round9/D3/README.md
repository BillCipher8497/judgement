# D3 representation and bounds

The game remains four players, three cards each, frozen myopic bids, and the original toy TA hero. Hero actions are fixed on **every** counterfactual branch in the tree. `compact_tree.py` stores the same play-only tree as `frozen_tree.py`, with numeric buffers and byte-packed private keys. The saved source-fingerprinted OS-temp pickle is only a derived construction cache; research summaries and profiles belong in `results/`.

`reachable_ta.py` prepares the **opponents' baseline** by traversing all chance roots under baseline play and each individual unilateral-deviation support. At a deviator's node it follows every action; at other nodes it computes and follows the original TA choice. Rows not reached by any of these traversals can be completed by any legal one-hot action. They have zero counterfactual reach in the baseline/individual-BR comparisons, so those values are exact. These rows are not claimed to reproduce TA at arbitrary histories with multiple simultaneous earlier deviations. The regression test compares baseline, private BR values and own-row BR policies, and the composed independent-BR heuristic with exhaustive TA completion.

In the NPZ, `fixed` is this baseline completion, **not** a fully enumerated TA table at unreachable histories. All spoiler rows are free in CFR and in the certificate; only bids and the hero have been collapsed as fixed choices. Thus the arbitrary baseline completion does not restrict the hidden-team search. The simultaneous independent-BR candidate uses the complete best-response policies returned by the solver, including their usual lowest-legal tie choices at zero-reach infosets.

The primary interval is [PI minimum, achieved feasible score]. It bounds an optimization problem, not sampling uncertainty. The optional `root_bound.py` relaxation constrains only the first free spoiler decision by private information and grants omniscience afterward; its separate proof is in ROOT_BOUND.md. A certificate is required for a zero joint gap. Neither small unilateral residuals nor multiplayer CFR updates alone prove team optimality. Complete chance enumeration uses no random seed or sampling CI; the resource pilot is not a substitute for that enumeration.
